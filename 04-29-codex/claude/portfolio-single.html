Luminara Residences

A luxury property platform built to move premium inventory with cinematic storytelling, stronger qualification flows, and a sharper global brand presence.

Client Luminara Group
Year 2024
Scope Strategy, UX, UI, Build
Result +61% qualified inquiries
Challenge

Elevate trust without slowing inquiry.

The previous site leaned too heavily on brochure-style visuals and buried the information serious buyers and intermediaries needed to move forward. The brand needed more authority, faster qualification, and a structure that could support international inventory.

Approach

Editorial pacing with commercial discipline.

We rebuilt the experience around property narratives, location context, and concise inquiry touchpoints. The interface kept the atmosphere premium while making floor plans, availability, and concierge outreach easier to access across devices.

Qualified Leads +61%
Session Depth +34%
Mobile Conversion +29%
